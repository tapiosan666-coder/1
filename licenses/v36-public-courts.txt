FIND YOURSELF v36 public courts

The cut-stone and ceramic artwork, botanical and architectural illustrations, bridge diagrams, suspended joinery, display frames and north garden access paving were drawn or constructed for this project. Generators for the printed graphics are provided in authoring/v36. The bridge silhouettes use the project's floor polygons. The three retail names in each wall artwork come from the project's locator and brand register.

The printed lettering was rasterized from Noto Sans CJK and Noto Serif CJK (SIL Open Font License 1.1, Google / Noto project) and DejaVu Sans (DejaVu / Bitstream Vera font licenses). These assets include only the rendered signage. Fonts previously included with the game retain their own notices. No photographic or commercial game assets were copied into these finishes.
