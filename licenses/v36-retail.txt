FIND YOURSELF v36 retail studios

The merchandise, joinery, dining booths, signage illustrations, display cases and entry finish artwork in assets/v36/studios were created for this project. Sources are in authoring/v36/retail. All store names on the prints derive from the existing 480-record project brand register. The models do not include third-party retailer logos or characters.

The models use the existing project texture assets/v10/wood-oak.png. Its inherited provenance and the CC0 SheenChair wood-map note are recorded in licenses/MODIFICATIONS-v10.txt. The department prints and threshold lettering use rasterized DejaVu lettering. The existing license is in licenses/DejaVu-copyright.txt.
