FIND YOURSELF v35 / C館南側回廊・LUMEVA TABLE

Original for this project: game geometry, baked lettering artwork, café menu layout,
and the editorial grain emblem. See c_promenade_v35.gd and authoring/v35_c_bakery.py.

The café menu incorporates assets/v17/photos/campaign_cafe.jpg, an original Blender
render from v17. The v17 shrub and ficus meshes use their inherited notices in
v17-original-assets.txt. Existing limestone, pavers, plaster, brick and oak finishes
reuse the licenses already included with this project's materials.

New raster typography: Noto Sans CJK and Noto Serif CJK, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
See Noto-OFL.txt. The two baked textures are included and require no extra fonts
or Python at runtime. Rebuilding the art source uses Pillow and the two Noto
font collections.
